## Main idea
"Follow the Rules of Life" (생활수칙 지키기) — a satirical mock-course that
teaches public manners/etiquette by exaggerating things you should **not** do
in public. The tone is comedic and over-the-top, e.g.:

- Cough directly in someone's face to make sure they know you're sick.
- Cover your mouth with your sleeve "for the sake of humanity."

The humor comes from presenting obviously bad (or absurdly literal) behavior
as if it were sincere advice, satirizing real etiquette lessons.
