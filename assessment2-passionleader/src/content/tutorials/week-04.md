## Activity

Sit in a quiet study space for ten minutes and log every sound above the
ambient floor — a chair scrape, a bag zip, a phone buzzing, a phone call —
along with whether anyone nearby reacted to it.

## Record your reaction

Note whether the loudest reaction you observed matched the loudest sound, or
whether a quieter, longer-running noise went unaddressed longer than a
louder, shorter one.

## Reflection

In your own words, explain why the lecture says a room will tolerate a
five-minute phone call before anyone shushes, but not a ten-second shush
without a much louder reaction to the shush itself.
