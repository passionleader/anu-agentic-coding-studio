## Activity

Watch a busy footpath, doorway, or escalator exit for a sudden stop —
someone checking a phone, reading a sign — and note how far back the
disruption propagates before it clears.

## Record your reaction

Estimate how many people were caught in the pile-up, and how many of them
likely never learned what caused it.

## Reflection

In your own words, explain why the lecture treats crowd flow as a
fluid-dynamics problem, and name one place where the analogy breaks down — a
fluid has no single person who chose to stop.
