## Activity

Observe a busy café, library, or shared space at peak time and note every
territorial claim to a seat or table that isn't backed by someone actually
sitting there right now — a jacket, a bag, a laptop left open.

## Record your reaction

Note how the same kind of claim reads differently in a mostly-empty room
versus a packed one, and whether anyone actually contested it.

## Reflection

Using this week's Bad manner / Good manner pair as a model, write your own
one-paragraph case study of a claim you observed, stating what made it read
as legitimate or not. This is good practice for the evidence
[Assignment 1](/assessments/assignment-1/) is due this week.
