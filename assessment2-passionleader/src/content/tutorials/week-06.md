## Activity

On public transport, or recalling a recent trip, note every instance of one
passenger annexing more space than their body needs — a bag on a seat,
spread legs, a wide pole grip.

## Record your reaction

Note whether the behaviour changed as the vehicle filled up, and how quickly
the person adjusted, if at all.

## Reflection

Compare this week's bag-on-the-seat case to Week 5's seat-saving jacket:
state one way they're alike and one way they're different. Bring your
strongest case study from Weeks 2 through 5 to the
[Midpoint Crit](/sessions/midpoint-crit/) this week.
