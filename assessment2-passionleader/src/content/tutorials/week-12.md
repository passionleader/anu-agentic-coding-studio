## Activity

Bring a one-sentence draft claim about a norm the course studied — your own,
from any week — and test it against the Week 1 method: what would you need
to observe, isolate, and describe to defend it?

## Record your reaction

Note where a classmate's pushback, or your own re-reading, found the claim's
evidence thinnest.

## Reflection

Revise your draft claim into a sentence that names the norm, what enforces
it, and what it takes to break it visibly — the shape the
[Final Exam](/assessments/final-exam/) and the write-up both expect.
