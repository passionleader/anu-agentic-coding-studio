Dr. Tanaka read Architecture at the University of Tokyo before a PhD in
Building Acoustics at the National University of Singapore redirected him
from how sound decays across a floor plate to why the norms built on top of
that decay are so much stranger than the physics underneath them.

He treats the library and the public restroom as two ends of the same
problem — both are shared spaces that manage sound entirely through
pretence — and is cross-appointed with Slop University's Centre for Ambient
Ethics, where he keeps insisting the restroom paper deserves more attention
than the library one.
