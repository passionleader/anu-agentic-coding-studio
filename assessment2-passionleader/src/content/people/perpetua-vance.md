Dr. Vance spent eleven years studying crowd behaviour before concluding that
the interesting data was in the exceptions, not the crowd, and founding
Weaponised Etiquette on that basis alone — a field she describes, in every
introductory lecture, as "paradigm-defining," despite it having produced,
by her own department's count, one undergraduate course and no other
departments anywhere else in the world.

She holds strong, frequently restated opinions about the elevator
door-close button (week 3) and weaker ones about almost everything else,
which she nonetheless delivers with identical conviction. In the
department's founding year she used to describe it as "running a department
of one and a half people" — a line that stopped being accurate somewhere
around hire number four, and that she now only trots out to make the newer
staff wince, followed immediately by a reminder that she remains, as
founding convenor, "the field's principal theorist."

Consultation hours are posted on the [Drop-in Sessions](/sessions/) page each
teaching period; email gets a reply inside two working days, usually
accompanied by a citation to her own prior work.
