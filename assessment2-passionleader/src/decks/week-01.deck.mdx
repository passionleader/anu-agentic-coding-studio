## What this course is not

- not a complaint about rude people
- not permission to actually harm anyone
- not a course about manners in the "be nice" sense

```comment
Get the disclaimer out of the way early and move on fast — dwelling on it
undercuts the deadpan tone the rest of the deck relies on.
```

---

## What this course is

A method, applied to one public space per week:

1. **Observe** the norm as it actually operates
2. **Isolate** the smallest deliberate act that breaks it
3. **Describe**, precisely, what happens next

## The bridge analogy

An engineer doesn't understand a bridge's load limit by reading the spec.

They understand it by finding out exactly how much weight makes it fail.

```notes
This is the closest thing to a "big idea" slide in the deck. Give it room —
don't rush into the next slide.
```
