Man builds entire homepage using only the Comic Sans font

Weird News · Tuesday, August 5, 1997

Local hobbyist Gary Fenwick has completed what he calls his "magnum opus": a seventeen-page personal homepage in which every word, heading, and copyright notice is set in Comic Sans MS.

"People said it couldn't be done," Fenwick said. "They said, surely even the legal disclaimer should be in Times. I disagreed."

Typography experts who previewed the site described it as "a bold statement" and "legally distinct from a ransom note, but only just." Fenwick says he has no plans to add a second font.
