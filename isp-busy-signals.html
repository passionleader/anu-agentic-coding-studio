Local ISP reports zero busy signals for third day running

Top Stories · Tuesday, August 5, 1997

NetLink Communications, the town's only dial-up provider, confirmed Monday that no customer has received a busy signal in over seventy-two hours — a company record.

"We added a second phone line," said a NetLink spokesperson. "It was a big investment, but we felt our four hundred subscribers deserved it."

Customers reached for comment described themselves as "cautiously optimistic" but "still keeping the redial button within reach, just in case."
