Visitor counter in small town reaches all the way up to 12

Weird News · Tuesday, August 5, 1997

The town of Lower Dweeble is celebrating after the visitor counter on its community homepage rolled over from 11 to 12 late Sunday night.

"We weren't sure we'd ever see double digits," said the site's webmaster, who runs the page from the back office of the general store. "Most of it was probably me, refreshing to check the weather page."

The mayor has announced plans to commission a small commemorative GIF to mark the occasion, to be added just above the counter itself.
