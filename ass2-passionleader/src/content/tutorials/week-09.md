## Activity

Pick one of this week's three revisited cases (elevator, café seat-saving,
transit space) and, without looking back at its original deck, write your
own one-paragraph Bad manner / Good manner pair from memory.

## Record your reaction

Compare what you wrote to the original deck's version. Note anything you
flattened, exaggerated, or left out — and whether the case still felt
contested once you'd written it down plainly.

## Reflection

State in a few sentences whether this week's method — isolate the act, name
what makes it deniable or contested, state a plain verdict — actually
generalised for the case you picked, or whether forcing it into that shape
lost something the original case had. [Assignment 2](/assessments/assignment-2/)
is due this week and rewards exactly this kind of honest self-check.
