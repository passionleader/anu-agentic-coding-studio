## Activity

Watch (or recall in detail) an elevator boarding sequence: who reaches for
the panel, who holds the door, and where each rider positions themselves
relative to the others.

## Record your reaction

If you can, time how long a held door actually costs the people already
inside, against how long waiting for the next car would cost the person who
didn't make it.

## Reflection

In your own words, explain why the lecture describes the elevator as the
"purest small-scale power structure" in the built environment — and how the
Bad manner / Good manner pair from this week's deck turns on exactly that
cost comparison.
