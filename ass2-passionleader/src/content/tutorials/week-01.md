This week's Drop-in Session (see [Orientation](/sessions/orientation/)) is
where the activity actually happens: watching how people queue for a shared
coffee machine and isolating the smallest deliberate act that breaks the
queue. This page covers the two things that go with it — what to record,
and what to take away.

## Activity

In small groups, watch a queue for a genuinely low-stakes resource — a coffee
machine, a shared printer, a water cooler — and note who cuts in, who lets
someone in, and how the people already waiting respond, if at all.

## Record your reaction

Write down exactly what you saw, in the order it happened: who did what, and
how the queue reacted. Resist the urge to summarise it as "someone was rude."
The method asks for the specific act, not the verdict.

## Reflection

In one paragraph, state the norm you think was operating, the smallest
deliberate act that broke it, and precisely what happened next. This is the
shape every case-study week from here on will take.
