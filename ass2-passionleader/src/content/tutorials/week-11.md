## Activity

Choose a technique from any earlier week and rehearse an exaggerated,
clearly-not-genuine version of it to perform in the
[Live Practicum](/sessions/live-practicum/)'s supervised session. Sign up
for a slot in advance through the session sheet posted before Week 9.

## Record your reaction

After performing, or watching a classmate perform, note the room's
real-time reaction — what people actually did, not what you expected them
to do.

## Reflection

State honestly what the exaggerated performance revealed about the norm
that a subtle, undetectable version wouldn't have.
