Halfway through the case-study run, this Drop-in Session is a group critique
rather than a new topic. Bring the case studies you've built from Weeks 2
through 5 (coughs, elevators, libraries, cafés) and present the strongest one
in five minutes: the norm, the isolated act that broke it, and what you
actually observed happen next.

The room — both convenors and your cohort — will push on where the evidence
is thin. This isn't marked, but it's the last checkpoint before Week 7's
queueing and threshold-litter case studies and the Practicum in Week 11, so
treat the pushback as the cheapest correction you'll get before those
count.
