This is the session the whole course has been building toward: you perform,
live and in front of your cohort, a deliberate and visibly exaggerated
version of a violation drawn from any earlier week — an elevator standoff
(with classmates playing the other riders), a seat-saving dispute (over a
seat a classmate has agreed to contest), a queue cut (in a line made
entirely of your cohort) — and the room reacts to it in real time. Everyone
on the receiving end has agreed in advance to be part of the session; this
is a performance for a consenting cohort, not a demonstration on the public
the earlier weeks studied. Mr. Osei supervises the session and runs the
debrief immediately afterward — it isn't separately graded, but the habit of
explaining exactly what you targeted and why is the same habit Assignment 2
and the Final Exam will expect from you.

Sign up for a ten-minute performance slot in advance through the session
sheet posted before Week 9. Come with your technique chosen and rehearsed —
this session is not the place to decide what you're performing.
